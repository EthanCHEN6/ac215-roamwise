Privacy and data

RoamWise is an AC215 classroom prototype. The pilot covers Boston, Cambridge and Somerville.

Your trip request

The Python server receives the preferences you submit. In the cloud deployment, the request and selected source passages are sent to Google's model service to prepare search queries and a draft itinerary. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Operational logs record model names, timing, token counts and dataset versions. The application does not log full prompts or store submitted trip preferences in a server database. The hosting and model providers may retain service logs under their own policies.

Saved trips

Saved trips stay in this browser's local storage. They are not synchronized to your group. Export a text copy to share. Remove saved trips with the delete control on the home page.

Historical sources

The retrieval index uses the Massachusetts subset of UCSD Google Local 2021, through September 2021. The pilot contains 500 places and sampled source passages. A source citation does not verify current opening hours or factual accuracy.

Current Google Maps lookups

When enabled, a current-details request uses the official Places API. Results are displayed separately with attribution. They are not inserted into the vector index or saved with your trip. The maintenance job stores only Google place IDs and our check timestamps.

Google Maps use is subject to the Google Maps terms and Google Privacy Policy. OpenStreetMap map tiles are supplied by the OpenStreetMap contributors.

Planning limits

AI explanations may contain errors. Visit durations and transfer times are estimates. Stop order is not a route-optimization guarantee. No reservations, purchases or messages are sent by this application.
